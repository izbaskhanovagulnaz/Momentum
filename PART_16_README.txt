MOMENTUM — PART 16: UNIVERSAL GOALS

Added:
- Create, edit and delete goals.
- Three tracking types: accumulative, measurement and checklist.
- Universal progress history with edit and delete.
- Automatic progress calculation.
- Checklist stages with edit, complete and delete.
- Motivation cover photo and progress photo gallery.
- Photo edit/delete and date/caption.
- Mobile bottom sheets.
- Firestore synchronization between phone and desktop.
- Migration of older goals and contribution records.

Photo safety:
Images are compressed before Firestore sync and the gallery is limited to five photos per goal to stay below the Firestore document limit.

DEPLOY:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Run deploy.bat.
4. Wait for DEPLOY COMPLETE.
5. Open Momentum and accept the in-app update.
